Pong

Two profiles. Two paddles. First to 10.

SingleplayerMultiplayer

Connecting to your profile...

How to play

Move using Up/Down arrows or hold the on-screen buttons. The host controls the left paddle and the guest controls the right. Score through the white goal in the middle half of the opponent’s end wall. Shots outside the goal bounce back. First to 10 wins. Either player can pause; the host resumes when both players are connected.
