A quick guide

Find your next game.

Play Doom, Snake, Circle Hop or Medieval RPS, save your progress, and compare local scores.

Start playing

Use Profile to create a unique username and password or sign in. Choose a playable game and New game to begin. RPS saves each round. Doom has a Save game button and saves every 10 seconds while alive. Wait for the Saved message before closing the tab. Continue restores your one saved game. Starting a new game replaces that save after confirmation while keeping your leaderboard best. Guests can play without saving. Snake and Circle Hop save their current state automatically and restore it paused. Click Resume when ready. Their leaderboards track high scores.

Use Tab to move between links and buttons. Press Enter to activate them, and Escape to close a menu.

Original Doom

Play the original shareware Episode 1 on Hurt Me Plenty. Click Start Doom or Continue saved game. WASD or arrows move, mouse or Ctrl fires, Space opens doors, Shift runs, 1–7 changes weapons, and Tab opens the map. Click the game to capture the mouse. Esc opens or closes the Doom menu and releases the mouse. The website Menu button also opens it. Use Up/Down to choose an option, Enter to select, Left/Right to adjust sliders, and Backspace to go back. Settings are stored with your profile checkpoint. Save game pauses and writes your one checkpoint. Resume continues play. A dead player does not overwrite your last living checkpoint. Use Load Game in Doom to return to it, or reload the page and Continue.

Controls: Keyboard and mouse

Snake

Use the arrow keys to change direction. Collect food to earn points. Crossing an edge brings you back on the opposite side in the same row or column. A black bomb appears after every 10th apple. Collect it to remove 1–5 tail squares, leaving at least three. Eat the next apple first and the bomb disappears until the next 10th apple. Bombs do not subtract score. Hitting your own body ends the run.

Controls: Arrow keys

Circle Hop

Press Space or tap the game area to jump. Jump over rectangles, triangles, circles and diamonds. The game gets slightly faster at 100, 200, 300 points and each following 100-point milestone. Touching a shape ends the run.

Controls: Space, Up arrow or tap

Medieval RPS

Each choice beats two others and loses to two. Identical choices tie. Sword beats Archer and Catapult. Archer beats Catapult and Dragon. Shield beats Sword and Archer. Catapult beats Shield and Dragon. Dragon beats Sword and Shield.

Local saves and leaderboards

Each profile has one save slot per game. RPS currently saves wins, losses, ties, streaks, and its last result after every round. Its leaderboard ranks best winning streaks. Doom saves your position, health, inventory, enemies, and current level. Its leaderboard ranks most kills in one level on the fixed Hurt Me Plenty difficulty. The Overall board adds placement points from each supported game: 10 for first, 9 for second, down to 1 for tenth. Ties share ranks and points. Snake saves its body, apple, active bomb, direction and apple score. Circle Hop saves its position, jump velocity, obstacle shapes, spawn countdown, speed and score. Pong and Memory Match boards will receive scores when those games are implemented.

Where your data lives

Use the same browser and address, including the port. Localhost and GitHub Pages have separate profiles and saves. Clearing browser data removes them. These are local profiles, not online accounts or secure protection from someone who controls the browser. Sign out on a shared computer. There is no password recovery.
