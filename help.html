A quick guide

Find your next game.

Play Medieval RPS, save your progress, and compare local scores.

Start playing

Use Profile to create a unique username and password or sign in. Choose Medieval RPS and New game to begin. Each round saves automatically. Continue restores your one saved game. Starting a new game replaces that save after confirmation while keeping your leaderboard best. Guests can play without saving.

Use Tab to move between links and buttons. Press Enter to activate them, and Escape to close a menu.

Local saves and leaderboards

Each profile has one save slot per game. RPS currently saves wins, losses, ties, streaks, and its last result after every round. Its leaderboard ranks best winning streaks. The Overall board adds placement points from each supported game: 10 for first, 9 for second, down to 1 for tenth. Ties share ranks and points. The other game boards will receive scores when those games are implemented.

Where your data lives

Use the same browser and address, including the port. Localhost and GitHub Pages have separate profiles and saves. Clearing browser data removes them. These are local profiles, not online accounts or secure protection from someone who controls the browser. Sign out on a shared computer. There is no password recovery.
